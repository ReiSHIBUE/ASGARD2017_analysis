Notes on methods:
• Bloom stage: from the median bloom index (= 1 − rescaled PC1 of nine standardized environmental variables). pre < 0.45, mid 0.45–0.55, post > 0.55.
• Alpha / Gamma / Bacteroidia (%): per-sample relative abundance of each class within the filtered ASV set, averaged across the samples of each cluster.
• Characteristic ASV: the ASV with the largest difference between its mean relative abundance inside vs outside the cluster.
• db-RDA significant: variables significant in the overall distance-based RDA (not cluster-specific).
• WM = water mass (AnW = Anadyr Water; wSW/cSW = warm/cold Shelf Water; wCW = warm Coastal Water; MWW = Modified Winter Water).
